Question: A 22-year-old male marathon runner presents with right-sided rib pain when he runs long distances. [ASSISTANT]
Explanation: Among the options, the pectoralis minor originates from the outer surfaces of the third through fifth ribs, so it can be used to help correct this rib dysfunction.

Question: A 36-year-old man has 3 weeks of low back pain. Prone examination reveals a deep sacral sulcus on the left, a posterior inferior lateral angle on the right, and a lumbosacral junction that springs freely on compression. Which diagnosis is most likely?

[ASSISTANT]
Explanation: A deep sulcus on the left, posterior ILA on the right, and a negative spring test indicate a right-on-right sacral torsion.
Answer: Therefore, the answer is (D) Right-on-right sacral torsion.

[ASSISTANT]

Answer: Therefore, the answer is (D) Phenoxybenzamine.
